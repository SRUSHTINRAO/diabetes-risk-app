🥗 Diet — What to Eat
🌾
Choose whole grains over refined Swap white rice and maida with brown rice, ragi, jowar or whole wheat. They digest slowly and keep blood sugar stable.
🥦
Fill half your plate with vegetables Leafy greens like spinach, methi and palak are especially good — low in carbs, high in fibre and nutrients.
🫘
Eat more legumes and pulses Dal, rajma, chana and moong are high in protein and fibre — they slow glucose absorption significantly.
💧
Drink water, not sugary drinks Replace chai with extra sugar, soft drinks and packaged juices with plain water, buttermilk or unsweetened nimbu pani.
🥜
Healthy snacking Choose a handful of nuts, roasted chana or fresh fruit over biscuits, namkeen or fried snacks.
🚫 Diet — What to Limit
🍚
White rice in large portions Eat smaller quantities — pair with dal and vegetables to slow absorption.
🧁
Sweets and mithai Especially avoid daily sweets — even natural ones like jaggery spike blood sugar.
🥤
Packaged fruit juices They contain as much sugar as cola — eat whole fruit instead.
🍟
Deep fried foods Samosas, pakodas and puri regularly increase BMI and insulin resistance.
🏃 Exercise — Move More
🚶
Walk 30 minutes every day The single most effective and accessible exercise for diabetes prevention. Even a brisk walk after dinner helps significantly.
🧘
Yoga twice a week Poses like Surya Namaskar, Paschimottanasana and Vrikshasana help regulate blood sugar and reduce stress hormones.
🏋️
Strength training helps too Muscle tissue uses glucose as fuel — more muscle means better blood sugar control. Simple bodyweight exercises work fine.
📵
Break sitting time every hour Stand up, stretch or walk for 5 minutes every hour if you have a desk job or study long hours.
🌙 Lifestyle Habits
😴
Sleep 7–8 hours every night Poor sleep increases cortisol and hunger hormones — both directly raise blood sugar levels and increase diabetes risk.
🧘
Manage stress actively Chronic stress raises blood glucose. Try meditation, deep breathing or even 10 minutes of quiet time daily.
🩺
Check your blood sugar annually Especially if you have a family history. Prediabetes has no symptoms — early detection is everything.
⚖️
Maintain a healthy weight Even losing 3–5 kg if you're overweight dramatically reduces your risk — you don't need to be thin, just healthier.
